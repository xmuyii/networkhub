🎯
Target placement & sniper scope
Requesters place targets on a map at their Google Business location. Reviewers scan through a scope — targets pulse when active. Lock on within the window, post the review, claim the bounty. The scope shakes slightly under competition pressure.
⬆️
Levelling system
New recruits get notification alerts so they earn faster. At Level 1 alerts stop — you hunt live. Level 2 (₦20,000 earned) unlocks Reviewer-Requester status: post your own social media quests for likes, comments, and shares.
🤖
AI verification layer
A bot listens to Google Business activity and matches posts to registered accounts. AI filters spam, copy-paste, negative or off-target reviews before payment releases. Login with Google account makes verification seamless.
🏆
Leaderboard — daily, weekly, all-time
Reviewer and requester leaderboards reset daily and weekly. All-time rankings unlock prestige skins and premium quests. Your kill-feed shows in real time as bounties are claimed.
💼
Dual wallet system
Requesters deposit and schedule review campaigns. Reviewers earn and withdraw. A requester can deposit ₦50,000 and drip ₦5,000 daily. Wallets are separated — requesters cannot withdraw, reviewers cannot deposit for review campaigns.
🛡️
Community reporting & keyword filters
Premium requesters define keywords and patterns — reviews that miss are auto-failed. Users can report exploitative businesses. After a threshold of verified reports, the business is queried and potentially banned.
